Where the weight goes

One standing humanoid, cut into its parts. Weights are the OpenG1 page’s masses times 9.81, every joint torque is 85% of its rated limit, and the floor pushes back with exactly what the robot weighs.

§ 04

Notes

— 04 / 04 —
N.01Arms

Arm joints carry the weight beyond them with the arm hanging, so shoulder yaw and wrist roll sit on vertical axes and take no gravity moment.

N.02Legs and waist

Levers use the weight above each joint, as in one-leg support, the worst case. Hip yaw and waist yaw are vertical axes.

N.03Waist group

Three waist-link masses from the page: 0.244, 0.047 and 9.598 kg. TBC the page lists 9.598 kg on the waist-pitch row only.

N.04Source

OpenG1 actuator build guide, torque and mass table (29-DOF). Not affiliated with Unitree.
